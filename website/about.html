Keel Labs

Keel Labs LLC makes Newzyx, a daily news podcast and website for curious kids and teens.

Each episode takes a handful of real stories from science, technology, sports, health, and the wider world, and explains them in a short, clear show. The goal is to help listeners understand what happened and why it matters, without the noise of a full news broadcast.

Newzyx is a product by Keel Labs LLC. The show is meant for listeners about 10 to 16 years old, and it works well for families listening together.

Questions about the show or this site: ryan@newzyx.com.
